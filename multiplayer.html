How to play
  • Share the room code. Both players press Ready to begin.
  • Poker: 3 energy. Play 1–5 cards, discard your hand, then end your turn.
  • Blackjack: 1 energy. Beat the dealer to deal damage. A loss triggers an attack; a push refunds the energy.
  • End turn: the Ogre attacks, then play passes to your teammate. Defeat the Ogre together.
