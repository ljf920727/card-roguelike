Juniper

Blackjack and poker, one run at a time.

How to play

Each run is four fights against random enemies. The last one is a boss. Your HP carries over between fights, and you recover a little after each win.

Your turn

You have 3 energy and a hand of 5 cards from a shuffled 52-card deck. Spend energy on poker hands or blackjack rounds, then end your turn and the enemy attacks for the amount it showed.

Blackjack

Deal (1 energy) to get 2 cards; the enemy gets 2, one face down. Hit to take another card, stand to stop, and the enemy draws until it reaches 17. Closest to 21 without going over wins.

Win and the enemy takes damage equal to your total (a natural blackjack deals 30). Lose or bust and the enemy hits you. A tie refunds your energy.

Poker

Select 1 to 5 cards from your hand. The best poker hand among them is played, from high card up to a royal flush. Bigger hands hit harder; pairs give shield and flushes heal. The full list is in the side panel.
